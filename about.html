About Us

PT Berkat Muri Sejahtera

An Indonesian trading company established in 2025 and based in Jakarta, focused on sourcing, aggregation and distribution.

Company overview

BMS connects two groups: suppliers who have agricultural and bio-based materials, and business buyers who need them. Suppliers can be individual farmers, farmer associations, collectors or other parties across Indonesia.

We aggregate supply from a growing base of suppliers, process it to the buyer's needs where required, and sell it to manufacturers, processors and industrial buyers.

The value BMS adds: suppliers get a single, accessible route to a buyer. Buyers get one company to ask for a material, instead of searching supplier by supplier.
